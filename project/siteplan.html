Final Project - WDD131

Site Name

Name: J&M Signature

Reason: I chose this name because it elegantly combines the initials of my wife and myself, representing our synergy as business and creative partners. Adding "Signature" reflects our commitment to delivering a unique, premium, and deeply personal touch to every event we document, establishing it as a highly professional brand identity.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to serve as a digital portfolio and service catalog. It will allow potential clients to view the quality of my previous event work, learn about my current rates, and streamline bookings through a direct contact inquiry form.

Scenarios

  • Scenario Question 1: What types of events or coverage options do you offer, and why should I choose to hire you over others?
  • Scenario Question 2: How can I get in touch with you to request a personalized quote for my upcoming event?

Color Schema

An elegant and organic color palette has been selected for this project to evoke sophistication and timelessness:

  • Primary Color (#1a1a1a): Jet Black used for backgrounds in headers and portfolio layouts to provide maximum contrast and make our visual work stand out.
  • Accent Color (#556b2f): Olive Green used for buttons, interactive links, structural borders, and key list styling to represent organic growth, prestige, and high-end aesthetics.

Typography

  • Headings (Playfair Display): A sophisticated Serif font to give headings an artistic, elegant, and high-end feel.
  • Body Text (Roboto): A clean, modern Sans-serif font to ensure high readability for descriptions, pricing, and forms.
